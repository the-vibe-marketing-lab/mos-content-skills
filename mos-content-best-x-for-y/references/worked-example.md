# Worked example: demand check and eligibility gate (labelled example, not a rule)

This is a real first run of the method, on The Vibe Marketing Lab (TVML), a free Skool
community that teaches marketers to build AI systems with Claude Code, with a $9 paid tier.
It shows what the shortlist stage looks like in practice. Nothing here is a default for other
brands.

## What was run (October 2026)

- Google Ads search volume for 38 candidate terms, US and AU, through DataForSEO.
- 10 unbranded buyer prompts plus 1 branded prompt, through the ChatGPT app, Google AI Mode
  and Perplexity, one run each (US). Total cost about US$0.33.

## What it found

- TVML was named in none of the 30 unbranded answers, but all three engines described it
  correctly when asked by name. The gap was in category answers, not brand recognition.
- Most terms had 10 or fewer searches a month and 14 returned nothing. The case for the work
  was the AI answers; volume was a tiebreaker.

## The gate in action

Passed (TVML is an honest pick for this Y):

- Best Claude Code skills for marketing (590 US / 70 AU for "best claude code skills")
- Best Claude Code course for marketers (110 US; a thin field, so the most winnable page)
- Best course to learn AI SEO and GEO
- Best AI training for SEO professionals
- Best Skool communities for AI marketing

Failed, and left off:

- Best AI course for beginners: 1,900 US, the biggest term, but it is a generalist need that
  HubSpot Academy, Google AI Essentials and Coursera serve better. Audience test failed.
- Best free AI course for marketers: the field is genuinely free, broad courses. Audience
  failed against the field.
- Best AI automation course for small business owners: Make and Zapier academies fit better.
  Offer and audience failed.
- Best AI course for marketing agency owners: partial fit. Parked as a possible later page
  that mostly recommends others and lists TVML only for SEO-led agencies.

## Watch-out it surfaced

Perplexity listed a different community with a near-identical name ("The Vibe Marketers").
A name collision like this is an entity-clarity risk worth fixing on the brand's AI info page,
not in the buyer's guide.

## The lesson for other brands

The biggest-volume Y was the one the brand should not target. The gate exists to stop a skill
(or a person) chasing volume onto pages where the brand would be a dishonest pick.
